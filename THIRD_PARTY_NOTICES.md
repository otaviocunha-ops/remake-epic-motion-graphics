# Toolkit provenance and licensing

The engine core, original engine utility scripts, nine prompts, playbook, source examples and GOJI specification were supplied by the repository owner from the Frame by Frame toolkit. Their original checksums are in references/toolkit-provenance.json. No license file was present in the supplied toolkit. Those files retain their original provenance and are **not** covered by this repository's MIT grant. Redistribution/relicensing permission must be established with their rights holder. The owner's request authorized packaging them, but does not establish an upstream open-source license.

The portable renderer/page template are adaptations; original versions remain in engine/upstream/. Those adaptations include upstream material and have the same unresolved upstream licensing scope. Playwright is Apache-2.0; NumPy uses its BSD license; Pillow uses its own permissive license; FFmpeg build licensing depends on the supplied binary. Dependencies are installed from their distributions, not vendored. The demo is newly authored procedural graphics and synthesized audio. No Nola assets, private product source, downloaded reference music or user footage are included.
